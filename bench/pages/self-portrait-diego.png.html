Fragments of Me

DIEGO RAMOS · GRADE 12 · MIXED MEDIA, DIGITAL COLLAGE · 2025

Concept

I made this portrait out of shapes because I do not think a person is one thing. In my own life I am a student, a goalie, a brother and a kid who spends a lot of time on a laptop. A single smooth portrait would not say that, so I broke my face into fragments and gave each piece a different color.

Process

I built it in a drawing program from a photo of myself, tracing over it with polygons and then removing the photo. The hard edges came from my love of poster design. I added the black lines last to hold the pieces together, which also made the face look more broken, which was kind of the point.

Reflection

My teacher said the composition is strong but the shading on the face is missing. I agree. I might try this again with paper cutouts so the pieces have real shadow. I want the next version to feel less like a poster and more like me.
