Catalase Lab

Sofia Marchetti — Bio 4th period

Purpose

The purpose of this lab was to see what happens to catalase when you change the temperature.

Hypothesis

I think that the hot one will work the best because heat makes things go faster.

Materials

Liver, hydrogen peroxide, test tubes, water bath, ruler

Procedure

We put liver in test tubes with peroxide at different temperatures. Then we measured the foam. We did it two times each.

Data

Temp Foam
cold 1
room 4
warm 6
hot 0

Conclusion

My hypothesis was wrong because the hot one did not work. Catalase is a protein that makes hydrogen peroxide so when it was hot it stopped making it and there was no foam. The warm one had the most foam so it worked the best. The cold one did not work as good because it was cold.

Some errors we might have made were that we measured wrong and the liver was different sizes. If we did it again we would be more careful.
