PHOTOSYNTHESIS

How plants turn sunlight into food · by Ravi Desai, Bio P3

6 CO2 + 6 H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6 O2

1. Light reactions

Happen in the thylakoids of the chloroplast.

  • Chlorophyll absorbs light
  • Water is split, releasing oxygen
  • Makes ATP and NADPH (energy carriers)

2. Calvin cycle

Happens in the stroma.

  • Uses CO2 from the air
  • Uses the ATP and NADPH from step 1
  • Builds glucose
Why does it matter? Almost every food chain starts with photosynthesis, and the oxygen we breathe comes from it. Leaves look green because chlorophyll reflects green light and absorbs red and blue. Plants also store extra glucose as starch, which is why potatoes are full of it!
