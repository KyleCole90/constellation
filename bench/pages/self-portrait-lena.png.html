“Between Two Rooms”

Lena Vogel · Grade 11 · Art 2
Acrylic on 18 × 24 in. canvas board

Artist statement

I painted this portrait after my family moved from Hamburg to Ohio in eighth grade. I am still two people, the girl who speaks German at home and the one who speaks English at school, and I wanted a picture that holds both without choosing.

The background is split into warm gold on top and a heavy brick red below. I think of the gold as the old house and the red as the new one. My face sits right at the line between them. I used a limited palette on purpose so the skin and the blue sweater would stand out.

My eyes are looking at the viewer straight on because I wanted to look confident, even though I did not feel it at the time. The green circles behind me are plants from my grandmother’s balcony. They are blurry because memories fade.

The hardest part was the hair. I mixed a dark brown with a little blue so it would not look flat and black. If I did it again I would work on the proportions of the neck, which I think are too long.
